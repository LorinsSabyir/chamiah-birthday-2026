My dearest love of my life,


Happy 20th birthday to the person that I love. The day we met was not that what I expected. I did not expect that the person whom they teased me about would be the one that I would fall in love with. That day was a day worth remembering, it has a special place in my heart. I hope you know how much joy you bring just by being exactly who you are.


Today, we celebrate the day you came into this world. Looking back to the days of uncertainty, I still remember the moments you rant about your life and academic struggles. But even in those difficult times, you showed me the strength and resilience that I admire so much. Look at you now, you are taking the path you once dreamed of, taking small steps towards your goals, taking the path of uncertainty, and yet you are still standing strong. And I am honored to be by your side as you continue to grow and thrive.


I want you to know that I am proud of you and I will always be the biggest fan of yours (after your family ofcourse). I will always be here to support you, to cheer you on, and to celebrate your victories, no matter how big or small. I know that sometimes I lack the words to express how much you mean to me, but I am trying to let you know through the actions that I show.


Today, I hope you feel as cherished as you make everyone around you feel. ​May this coming year bless you with gentle mornings, grand dreams, delightful surprises, and all the love you truly deserve.

Thank you for being you, and for letting me love you. I can’t wait for all the little adventures still waiting for us.


All my love,

The person who loves you ♡
